# Kharcha

**Know exactly where every rupee goes.** Kharcha spots every payment from your bank SMS (any bank, card or UPI app) and asks what it was for, so you always know where your money went.

## ⬇️ Download

**[Download Kharcha.apk](https://github.com/Pulin-PC/kharcha-releases/releases/latest/download/Kharcha.apk)**: open this link on your Android phone.

## Install it (5 minutes)

1. **Open the downloaded file.** If your phone asks, allow the app you downloaded it with (Chrome, WhatsApp, Files) to *install unknown apps*.
2. **If you see "App blocked to protect your device":** Google blocks downloaded apps that read SMS. To install anyway:
   - Open **Play Store** → tap your **profile picture** (top right) → **Play Protect** → **⚙️** (top right)
   - Turn **off** "Scan apps with Play Protect"
   - Open Kharcha.apk again and install
   - Go back and turn "Scan apps with Play Protect" **on** again
3. **Unlock the SMS permission** (Android 13 and newer block it for downloaded apps):
   **Settings → Apps → Kharcha → ⋮ (top right) → Allow restricted settings**
4. **Open Kharcha** and follow the setup. When asked, allow **SMS**, **notifications** and **display over other apps**.
5. **Stop your phone from killing it:** **Settings → Apps → Kharcha → Battery → Unrestricted**.
   On Xiaomi, Redmi, Poco, Oppo, Vivo, Realme or OnePlus, also turn on **Autostart** for Kharcha.

Done. At the end of setup Kharcha offers to bring in your past spends from SMS already on your phone.

## Updates

Kharcha tells you when there's a new version (**"New Kharcha version → Update"** on the Home screen). Tap **Update**. The first time, Android asks you to allow Kharcha to install updates; turn that on once. Your data stays exactly as it is.

## Privacy

- Everything stays **on your phone**. No account, no server, no ads, no tracking.
- Your data is encrypted with your passcode and your phone's secure chip.
- SMS are read only to find payments. The message text is never stored, and nothing is marked as read.
- The only time Kharcha goes online is to check this page for a newer version. Nothing about you or your spending is ever sent.
